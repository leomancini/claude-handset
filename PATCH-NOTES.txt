Claude Handset  #3cf75256  -  hand-patched Gerber export
=========================================================
These Gerbers were edited directly (the Flux project was NOT changed) to fix the
DRC faults Flux reported. Coordinates below are mm from the board centre, the same
origin as the IPC-D-356 netlist. Original files are byte-identical to the Flux
export except for the items listed here.

1. 3V3 shorted to 3V3_PWRGD (Flux "Overlapping Copper", Through-hole - 1)
   - Removed the 3V3 via at (-1.50, 4.00). It sat on the PWRGD trace and also
     overlapped the second 3V3 via 0.35 mm away.
   - Kept the 3V3 via at (-1.42, 3.66); its pad is now 0.5 mm (0.3 mm drill) on
     all four layers so it clears the PWRGD trace by 0.16 mm.
   - Deleted the three 0.1 mm top-layer feeder segments to the removed via and
     added a 0.15 mm segment from the existing 3V3 trace end (-1.83, 3.41) to the
     kept via.

2. 3V3 open (Flux "Airwire" 3V3, traces 7d11 / dcde)
   - The group feeding RP2040 VREG_VIN, USB_VDD, ADC_AVDD and three IOVDD pins
     (with C7, C8, C9, C13, C15, C16) was isolated.
   - Added a 3V3 via at (-12.79, -8.20) on the existing 3V3 trace just below
     FB1 pin 1, and a 0.254 mm inner-layer-1 trace from it to the existing 3V3
     via at (-9.16, -5.86). The ground planes on In1, In2 and Bottom are cleared
     0.2 mm around the new via and trace (negative-polarity LPC objects at the end
     of each copper file); the top ground fill is cleared around the via too.
   - Deleted the redundant top-layer 3V3 trace (-4.29,-4.00)-(-6.09,-4.00)-
     (-6.24,-1.47) between C10 and C14 (both ends are fed elsewhere) to make room
     for item 3.

3. GND opens (Flux "Airwire" GND, C10:P2 / C14:P2, traces 549b / ee7e, and the
   "Fill: None" items)
   - RP2040 exposed pad had no ground connection at all: added four 0.6 mm GND
     vias inside it at (-7.30, 1.65), (-4.90, 1.70), (-6.00, 2.60), (-6.00, 0.70).
   - C10:P2 and C14:P2: added a GND via at (-5.80, -4.10) with 0.25 mm links into
     both pads.
   - C13:P2 / C16:P2: added a 0.5 mm-pad GND via at (-8.50, -6.40) with a 0.3 mm
     link from the end of their wide stub at (-8.23, -6.06).

4. Drill file and IPC-D-356 netlist updated to match (7 vias added, 1 removed,
   one via pad size changed). Solder mask / paste files untouched: all vias in
   this export are tented, and no via was placed inside an SMD pad.

Verification (script-based, pure geometry from the Gerbers + netlist):
   - every net is one copper island; no island carries two nets; no floating copper
   - no new copper-to-copper spacing below 0.127 mm was introduced; the deleted
     0.1 mm feeders removed the worst pre-existing spacing (0.044 mm)
   - JLCPCB 4-layer limits used: 0.09 mm spacing, 0.15 mm via hole, 0.25 mm via pad

Not fixed (pre-existing, outside the DRC list): several spacings under 0.09 mm
remain from the original layout, e.g. USB-C pads vs traces at 0.050 mm around
(-0.25, -21.3), MCU_RUN pad vs SWDIO trace 0.069 mm at (-7.8, 5.4), F13/F14 button
traces vs 3V3 near the RP2040 east pins 0.069-0.089 mm.

Renders of the edited areas are in patch-renders/ (orig_* = before, site_* = after;
red = top, green = In1, blue = In2, orange = bottom).

Patch 2 - decoupling capacitors moved next to the pins they serve
================================================================
   - C24 (100 nF, VBUS_5V) moved from (7.0, -0.5) to (12.3, -4.6), directly below the
     MAX98357A VDD pins; its VBUS pad sits on the existing VDD trace, its GND pad on the
     top ground fill. Old pads and the two feeder segments removed. 1.18 mm from the pin.
   - C11 (100 nF, 1V1_CORE) moved from (-0.5, -3.0) to (-6.45, 6.95) and changed to an 0402
     (Samsung CL05B104KO5NNNC, LCSC C1525) so it fits between the RP2040's north pin row and
     the crystal. Its 1V1 pad is fed straight up from pin 23 (DVDD); its GND pad links to the
     crystal's GND pad. 1.07 mm from the pin. The BOMs and pick-and-place carry the new
     footprint. Old pads and five feeder stubs removed.
   - C6 (100 nF, 3V3) moved from (-1.5, 6.8) to (-2.3, 6.9), the closest an 0603 can get to
     RP2040 pin 22 (north IOVDD) without rerouting the crystal: 3.45 mm instead of 4.15 mm.
     Its 3V3 pad picks up the existing 3V3 stubs, its GND pad sits on the ground fill.
   - To make room for C11, pin 22's 3V3 feed now leaves the pad diagonally to x = -5.85 and
     the 3V3 via that fed it moved from (-6.19, 7.27) to (-5.75, 7.27) with a 0.5 mm pad;
     the bottom-layer 3V3 trace was redrawn to end on the moved via.
   - Top ground fill cleared 0.15 mm around every relocated non-GND pad and along the new
     top traces (negative-polarity objects at the end of f_cu); plane clearances added on
     the inner/bottom layers for the moved via. Mask and paste openings follow the pads;
     silkscreen body marks moved with the parts (the C24 label moved, the C6 and C11 labels
     were left where they were).
   - Re-verified: no shorts, no opens, no new spacing below 0.127 mm; wiring simulator
     142/143 with the remaining note being C6 at 3.45 mm.
   Renders: patch-renders/caps_north_*.png and caps_amp_*.png. Files before patch 2 are in
   the session scratchpad only; original-flux-export/ still holds the untouched export.

Gerber syntax note (after patch 2)
----------------------------------
   The patch blocks had deleted all file attributes (%TD*%) before the export's own trailing
   %TD.AperFunction*%, which a strict RS-274X parser rejects as deleting an undefined attribute.
   A %TA.AperFunction,Conductor*% line was added before that trailer in the four copper files.
   All 12 files now parse cleanly with an independent parser (gerbonara 1.x); the negative-
   polarity clearances render as intended there too. jlcpcb/ holds the upload pack.
